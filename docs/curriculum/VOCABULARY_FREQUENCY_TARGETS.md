# Vocabulary Frequency Targets & Distribution Framework

This specification defines Zipfian frequency distributions and core headword targets across UniLang's 20 supported languages.

## 1. Frequency Distribution by CEFR Level

- **Tier 1 (Top 500 lemmas):** High-utility survival vocabulary, pronouns, auxiliary verbs, and cardinal numbers.
- **Tier 2 (Top 1,500 lemmas):** Essential conversational verbs, concrete nouns, common adjectives, and modal particles.
- **Tier 3 (Top 3,500 lemmas):** Abstract descriptors, workplace expressions, cultural discourse vocabulary, and idioms.

## 2. Thematic Cluster Taxonomy

Curriculum headwords are partitioned into 12 thematic clusters:
1. Greetings & Etiquette
2. Travel & Navigation
3. Culinary & Dining
4. Health & Medical
5. Housing & Accommodation
6. Professional & Business
7. Family & Social Relationships
8. Shopping & Currency
9. Emergency & Assistance
10. Arts, Literature & Traditions
11. Weather & Geography
12. Digital & Technology
