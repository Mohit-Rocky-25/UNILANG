# UniLang 🌍
### Learn Languages. Discover Cultures. Connect with the World.

UniLang is an all-in-one language-learning and cultural-discovery platform designed to help people learn new languages, understand different cultures, improve pronunciation, communicate confidently, and translate languages in real-world situations.

Unlike traditional language-learning applications that focus primarily on vocabulary and grammar, UniLang combines structured lessons, AI-powered translation, pronunciation practice, cultural exploration, personalized learning, and conversational experiences in one unified platform.

**Our vision:** Make language learning practical, personalized, culturally aware, and accessible to everyone.

## 🌐 About UniLang

Language is more than vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation. It is a way to understand people, traditions, perspectives, and cultures.

Learning a new language can help someone travel confidently, study abroad, communicate professionally, reconnect with their heritage, or build meaningful relationships across borders.

However, learners often need separate applications for lessons, translation, pronunciation, dictionaries, cultural information, and speaking practice.

UniLang aims to bring these experiences together in a single, intuitive platform.

The platform helps users progress from learning basic words to understanding context, practicing conversations, exploring cultural differences, and using their knowledge in everyday situations.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Language learners commonly face several challenges:

- **Fragmented learning:** Lessons, translation tools, dictionaries, and cultural resources are spread across different applications.
- **Limited real-world practice:** Learners may understand written language but struggle to speak naturally.
- **Lack of cultural context:** Literal translations often fail to explain politeness, tone, idioms, and social expectations.
- **Generic learning experiences:** Many learners need different study plans depending on their goals, proficiency, and available time.
- **Pronunciation difficulties:** Learners need opportunities to listen, repeat, and improve without feeling embarrassed.
- **Poor vocabulary retention:** Words learned today can be forgotten without effective revision.
- **Communication barriers:** Travelers, students, and professionals need quick and understandable translations in practical situations.

UniLang addresses these challenges through a unified learning and communication experience.

## 💡 Our Solution

UniLang combines language education, translation, cultural discovery, and practical communication tools into one platform.

Users can:

- Learn a language through structured lessons.
- Translate text between supported languages.
- Understand the meaning and context of unfamiliar expressions.
- Practice pronunciation and conversational skills.
- Explore cultural traditions, customs, history, and regional differences.
- Build personal vocabulary collections.
- Revise previously learned material.
- Track progress and receive personalized learning recommendations.
- Prepare for travel, education, professional communication, and everyday interactions.

The goal is not simply to help users translate words, but to help them understand and use languages more confidently.

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. Personalized Language Learning

A structured learning experience designed around the user's selected language, proficiency, goals, and progress.

Features:

- Beginner-to-advanced learning paths.
- Vocabulary-building exercises.
- Grammar lessons with practical examples.
- Reading and listening comprehension.
- Writing and sentence-building exercises.
- Pronunciation practice.
- Contextual examples and explanations.
- Progress tracking.
- Personalized lesson recommendations.
- Revision exercises based on previous performance.
- Adjustable lesson difficulty and session length.

Where appropriate, learning paths can align with recognized proficiency frameworks such as CEFR.

### 2. Smart Language Translator

A translation workspace designed to help users understand meaning rather than merely convert text.

Features:

- Text translation between supported languages.
- Automatic source-language detection where available.
- Source and target language selection.
- Alternative translations.
- Formal and informal wording.
- Professional and conversational translation styles.
- Contextual explanations.
- Idiom and expression interpretation where supported.
- Sentence-by-sentence breakdowns.
- Copy and share functionality.
- Saved translations and searchable history.
- Clear handling of ambiguous or unsupported language pairs.

The translator should preserve meaning and context as much as possible and communicate uncertainty when appropriate.

Machine-generated translations must not be represented as certified legal, medical, or professional translations.

### 3. Pronunciation and Speaking Practice

Help users develop confidence in speaking a new language.

Features:

- Audio pronunciation for words and phrases.
- Repeat-after-me exercises.
- Adjustable playback speed where supported.
- Speech recognition where available.
- Pronunciation feedback and suggested improvements.
- Listening exercises for similar sounds.
- Everyday conversation practice.
- Interactive speaking exercises.
- Scenario-based role-play.

Speech recognition and pronunciation feedback can vary by accent, microphone quality, and environment. Feedback should be presented as guidance rather than a definitive judgment.

### 4. Culture Explorer

Explore the cultural context behind languages.

Topics can include:

- Greetings and etiquette.
- Politeness and forms of address.
- Festivals and celebrations.
- Food and culinary traditions.
- Music, cinema, literature, and art.
- History and the evolution of language.
- Regional expressions and dialects.
- Gestures and social conventions.
- Workplace and academic communication.
- Idioms, humor, and expressions.
- Travel customs and everyday situations.

Culture guides should explain regional and individual variation rather than treating an entire country or community as culturally uniform.

Where possible, articles should include credible references and identify their geographical and historical context.

Help users collect, organize, and retain useful words.

Features:

- Definitions and example sentences.

- Spaced-repetition revision.
- Difficulty labels.
- Topic-based categories.
- Personal notes.
- Review history.
- Vocabulary progress tracking.

### 6. Grammar Coach

Make grammar easier to understand through clear explanations and examples.

Features:

- Word-order practice.
- Common mistake explanations.
- Contextual sentence correction.
- Comparisons between languages where linguistically appropriate.
- Recommended exercises based on learning progress.

Give users a low-pressure environment to practice real-world conversations.

Possible scenarios include:

## 🧭 Learning Experience

UniLang should make it easy for a user to begin learning without overwhelming them with too many features.

### Onboarding

1. Choose a language to learn.
2. Select a proficiency level or complete a placement assessment.
3. Choose a learning goal.
4. Set an optional weekly target.
5. Select a preferred session length.
6. Complete a short introductory lesson.
7. Receive a personalized learning path.

Possible goals include travel, education, work, family, cultural exploration, and general language proficiency.

### Example Daily Learning Session

| Duration | Activity | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| 3 minutes | Vocabulary revision | Strengthen recall |
| 5 minutes | New lesson | Learn a concept |
| 3 minutes | Listening and pronunciation | Improve spoken familiarity |
| 3 minutes | Conversation practice | Apply knowledge |
| 1 minute | Review and feedback | Identify next steps |

This is an example session, not a mandatory learning schedule.

### Learning Cycle

**Learn → Listen → Practice → Receive Feedback → Review → Apply in Real Life**

Translation and cultural explanations should complement this cycle and help learners become more independent over time.

```text
                  UNILANG WEB APPLICATION
        ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
        │ Learn · Translate · Speak        │
        │ Culture · Vocabulary · Progress  │
        └────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                         │ HTTPS
        ┌────────────────▼─────────────────┐
        │            BACKEND API            │
        │ Authentication and Authorization │
        │ Learning and Progress Management │
        │ Translation and AI Orchestration │
        │ Validation and Rate Limiting      │
        └───────────┬────────────┬─────────┘
                    │            │
           ┌────────▼─────┐  ┌───▼─────────────┐
           │  PostgreSQL  │  │ Provider Adapters│
           │              │  │ Translation      │
           │ Users        │  │ AI Models        │
           │ Lessons      │  │ Speech and Audio │
           │ Progress     │  └──────────────────┘
           │ Vocabulary   │
           └──────────────┘
```

### Architectural Principles

- Keep provider integrations replaceable through clearly defined interfaces.
- Validate inputs on the server.
- Enforce authorization for every user-owned resource.
- Store API keys and secrets on the server.
- Use timeouts, retries where appropriate, and clear error handling.
- Apply rate limits to expensive or abuse-prone endpoints.
- Track provider usage and costs.
- Cache data only when privacy, correctness, and provider terms permit.
- Provide useful fallbacks when external providers fail.
- Separate cultural and learning content from application logic where practical.

## 🤖 AI Integration

AI should improve the learning experience rather than be added simply for marketing.

Potential AI-assisted capabilities include:

- Context-aware translation.
- Grammar explanations.
- Alternative sentence suggestions.
- Personalized lesson recommendations.
- Conversation simulations.
- Vocabulary extraction from user-provided text.
- Feedback on written exercises.
- Explanations of expressions and idioms.

### AI Design Principles

- Identify AI-generated responses where appropriate.
- Explain uncertainty when context is insufficient.
- Avoid fabricating cultural or linguistic facts.
- Preserve the user's intended meaning.
- Apply request limits and usage controls.
- Keep provider credentials on the server.
- Review provider data-retention and training policies.
- Allow users to control saved content and history.
- Provide understandable error messages when a provider is unavailable.

Translation and AI quality should be tested across supported language pairs rather than assumed to be equally good for every language.

## 🔐 Security and Privacy

UniLang may process private messages, voice recordings, travel plans, and professional communication. Security and privacy should be part of the architecture from the beginning.

### Security Requirements

- HTTPS in production.
- Secure authentication and session management.
- Server-side secret storage.
- Input validation and output encoding.
- Authorization checks for user-owned records.
- Rate limiting and abuse prevention.
- Protection against injection, cross-site scripting, and cross-site request forgery where applicable.
- Safe file and audio handling.
- Dependency scanning and timely security updates.
- Logging that avoids unnecessary personal data and secrets.
- Appropriate backups and recovery procedures.
- Clear reporting procedures for security vulnerabilities.

### Privacy Principles

- Collect only the data necessary for the feature.
- Explain how user text and audio are processed.
- Review external provider data-handling policies.
- Offer controls for deleting saved content and history.
- Avoid storing sensitive content without a justified purpose.
- Document retention periods.
- Do not expose one user's saved material to another user.

Machine translation must not be marketed as a certified substitute for qualified professional translation in high-stakes contexts.

## 🌏 Accessibility and Localization

UniLang should aim for WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility where feasible.

Requirements include:

- Keyboard-accessible navigation.
- Visible focus indicators.
- Screen-reader-friendly labels.
- Readable typography and sufficient contrast.
- Captions or transcripts where appropriate.
- Support for long text and text expansion.
- Proper language metadata.
- Right-to-left text support for relevant languages.
- Suitable font fallbacks for different writing systems.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Adjustable audio speed where supported.
- Clear errors and accessible form validation.

The interface language and the language being learned must be separate settings. Changing one should not unexpectedly change the other.

## 🧪 Testing and Quality Assurance

Test complete user workflows, error cases, security boundaries, and accessibility.

### Learning Tests

- Lessons load for the correct language and proficiency level.
- Progress is saved and restored.
- Review scheduling works correctly.
- Users cannot access another user's learning records.

### Translation Tests

- Source and target language selections are respected.
- Empty and oversized inputs are handled safely.
- Unsupported language pairs are identified.
- Provider errors and timeouts produce useful feedback.
- Private translation history is protected.

### Speech Tests

- Microphone permission failures are handled.
- Users can cancel or retry recording.
- Audio controls are accessible.
- Audio retention follows the documented policy.

### Culture Tests

- Content displays appropriate attribution.
- Regional filters work correctly.
- Content feedback can be submitted safely.
- Unsupported cultural claims are not presented as verified facts.

### Security and Reliability Tests

- Authentication and session expiry.
- Authorization and ownership checks.
- Rate limiting.
- Input validation.
- Secret leakage checks.
- Dependency and build checks.
- Database migration tests.
- Backup and recovery checks.

### Definition of Done

A feature is complete when:

1. Expected behavior is documented.
2. Normal, error, and edge cases have been considered.
3. Security and privacy requirements are addressed.
4. Relevant automated tests pass.
5. Responsive behavior and accessibility are reviewed.
6. Documentation matches the implementation.
7. Remaining limitations are clearly communicated.

## 📊 Success Metrics

UniLang should measure whether users learn effectively and accomplish their goals.

### Learning Outcomes

- Lesson completion rate.
- Vocabulary recall after a defined interval.
- Improvement in assessment scores.
- Practice-session completion.
- Learner-reported confidence in real-world situations.

### Usability

- Time required to complete the first lesson.
- Success rate for common translation tasks.
- Ease of finding and saving useful phrases.
- Task completion across supported devices.
- Accessibility issues identified and resolved.

### Reliability and Cost

- API error and timeout rates.
- Translation and audio response latency.
- Cost per completed AI-assisted activity.
- Failed learning-progress writes.
- Provider availability and fallback success.

### Trust and Content Quality

- User-reported translation errors.
- Cultural-content corrections.
- Feedback usefulness ratings.
- Privacy and security incidents.

Metrics should be defined consistently and compared against measured baselines. Do not claim learning improvements or translation accuracy without supporting evidence.
